Standard Operating Procedures

Emergency Action Protocol

Follow these five universal steps whenever a disaster strikes your campus.

01
🔔

Alert & Warn

Activate emergency alarm system immediately. Use PA announcements, SMS alerts, and designated warning signals.

02
🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Assemble & Evacuate

Move to pre-designated assembly points. Teachers lead students. Account for all individuals in the roll call.

03
📞

Communicate

Contact emergency services (112). Report status to incident commander. Inform parents and administration.

04
🩺

First Aid & Triage

Trained CERT members provide immediate first aid. Prioritize casualties. Await professional medical support.

05
📋

Document & Recover

Record all actions taken, injuries, and damages. Begin incident debrief. Initiate psychological first aid.
